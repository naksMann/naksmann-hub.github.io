About Me

I'm Nakedi Tumelo Peta, a Computer Systems Engineering graduand at Tshwane University of Technology who just wrapped up a twelve-month Cybersecurity Internship at the 4IR Lab in Soshanguve. My work spans SOC monitoring, penetration testing, and ICS/OT security.

In the SOC I triaged logon and process-creation events in Microsoft Sentinel and Sysmon, documenting playbooks for the next analyst. On the offensive side, I ran a black-box pentest that chained an unrestricted file upload into remote code execution, and assessed a Siemens S7-1200 PLC by issuing an unauthenticated STOP command that halted it mid-run.

Outside of my work, I build my own tooling: a Wazuh SOC lab with anomaly detection, an Azure honeypot, and an Intrusion-based AI-facial recognition system.
